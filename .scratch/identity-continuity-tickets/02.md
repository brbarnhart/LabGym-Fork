## What to build

On a new Detect + track run, identity association uses Hungarian matching to constant-velocity predicted centers of mass, and unmatched identity slots park instead of teleporting to a dummy far-away center. Chasing and ordinary crossings keep identity slots. An animal that leaves the frame or is missed does not snap onto another animal across the arena.

Always on; no Detect + track checkbox. Still no occlusion freeze, split-detection rule, or Review IDs proposals.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Live association minimizes assignment cost to predicted centers of mass (last center plus last step), not greedy last-center-of-mass.
- [ ] A synthetic two-animal chase (close, not piled) keeps identity slots through the pursuit.
- [ ] An unmatched slot is parked: it does not bind to a detection that is a better match to an active slot and does not teleport to a dummy far-away center.
- [ ] After present animals are matched, a leftover detection may claim a parked slot only if it is not already a better match to someone active.
- [ ] Animals per kind remains a hard maximum; extra detections still do not create identity slots.
- [ ] Association stays independent per animal kind.
