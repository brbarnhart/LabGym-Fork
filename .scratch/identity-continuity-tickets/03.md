## What to build

The live pass handles the two remaining association failures in raw tracklets.

Split detection: two (or more) overlapping outlines that belong to one animal while another identity slot of that kind is unmatched must not steal. That 1–2 frame hiccup is a gap, not a lasting swap.

Occlusion bout: association freezes when the composite occlusion tests fire (fattened-outline intersection, small raw-contour gap, collapsed detection area next to another detection, or unmatched slot with a leftover detection nearby). Raw outlines need not intersect. Mere center-of-mass closeness is not freeze (that is chase, already handled). During freeze, last center of mass and velocity do not update off the pile. At freeze-lift, rematch uses the last pre-freeze identities.

Typical wrestle outlines almost touch. Keep the hidden-animal / one-blob clauses anyway.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Split detection (raw outline overlap + unmatched slot) does not assign both overlapping detections to two identity slots; the missing animal’s slot parks for those frames.
- [ ] A one- or two-frame split detection does not leave a lasting identity swap after both animals are detected again.
- [ ] Almost-touching, non-intersecting outlines of two piled animals produce an occlusion bout and freeze.
- [ ] A fully hidden animal (one leftover detection, a slot unmatched) is an occlusion bout, not proximity.
- [ ] Close centers of mass without occlusion tests (chase) do not freeze.
- [ ] At freeze-lift, rematch uses last pre-freeze identities, not the pile center. Intra-bout identities after each lift are correct on a synthetic wrestle with at least one mid-bout separation.
- [ ] Several freeze-lifts in one wrestle rematch at each lift in the live pass (not only at the final exit).
